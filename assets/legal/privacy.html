Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026 · Bolt of Aether
Also published at boltofaether.com/privacy-policy.html

This policy explains what Bolt of Aether (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. The app works offline for daily bolts, rituals, and your personal library. A network connection is used only for optional measurement, push delivery, and the in-app Privacy / Support pages.

Data stored on your device

The following stays on your iPhone unless you delete it or reset the app:

  • Bolts you write, save, or skip, plus your streak and visit history
  • Morning Charge and Evening Discharge entries, including optional photos and short voice notes
  • Nickname and optional profile photo
  • Preference toggles (haptics, motion, ritual reminders)

Photos and voice notes never leave the device unless you export them yourself. Uninstalling the app, or using Reset all data in Settings, permanently removes this local store.

Device and advertising identifiers

On iOS we may ask for permission, through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, to use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

  • If you allow: the identifier may be used to measure which marketing campaign brought you to the app and may be shared with our measurement partner, AppsFlyer.
  • If you decline or skip: we do not read or send the advertising identifier. Every feature of the app still works.

You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Third-party services

  • AppsFlyer — install and campaign measurement. When tracking is allowed, AppsFlyer may process the advertising identifier, AppsFlyer UID, locale, and conversion metadata. When tracking is denied, advertising identifiers are disabled; the SDK may still receive a first-party install signal without IDFA.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — optional push notifications. If you grant notification permission we send a device push token and our Firebase project id so we can deliver alerts you opted into. We do not use Firebase Analytics or ads in this app.
  • Apple — push delivery (APNs) and the tracking permission dialog.

These providers process data only to run the services above. We do not sell your personal information.

Technical information

When the app is online it may also send: iOS version (to build a compatible user agent), locale, bundle identifier, and IP address as seen by our servers or the providers above. We use this for security, diagnostics, and campaign measurement — not to build a marketing profile of your bolts or rituals.

How we use information

  • Provide the daily-bolt and ritual experience on-device
  • Measure app installs and campaign performance (AppsFlyer)
  • Deliver optional push notifications you allow
  • Keep the app stable and comply with law

Retention

On-device data stays until you delete it. Measurement and push tokens are kept only as long as needed to operate those services or as required by the provider’s own retention rules.

Your rights

You may request access or deletion of personal data we hold by emailing support@boltofaether.com. Local-only data is removed by uninstalling the app or using Reset all data. Depending on where you live, GDPR or similar laws may give you additional rights.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
